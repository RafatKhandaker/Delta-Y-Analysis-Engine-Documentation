## ⚡ What is EdgeFit?
**EdgeFit** (`edgefit.app`) is an **Agentic AI-powered social fitness, workout consistency, and macro tracking platform**. Unlike static workout apps that leave you with raw spreadsheets and generic templates, EdgeFit runs on the **Delta Y Analysis Engine (ΔY)**—an autonomous multi-agent intelligence layer that analyzes your daily inputs and continuously adapts your training and nutrition roadmap.

### Core Capabilities
* **Autonomous ΔY Diagnostics:** Evaluates cumulative progressive overload, calorie/macro adherence, and recovery readiness to eliminate plateaus before they happen.
* **Multi-Agent Architecture:** Powered by four specialized AI teammates working in sync:
  * **Programming Agent:** Dynamically adjusts sets, reps, load targets, and deload cycles based on real performance telemetry.
  * **Nutrition Engine:** Generates personalized macro targets and synthesizes remaining-macro meal plans tailored to your body composition goals.
  * **Delta Y Diagnostics Agent:** Detects fatigue accumulation, metabolic adaptation, and strength divergence across training blocks.
  * **Social Graph Agent:** Benchmarks your consistency and progression metrics against anonymized **Gold-Layer** social cohorts.
* **Medallion Data Lake Telemetry:** Processes raw workout logs, meal entries, and structured daily biometric & sleep assessment forms through encrypted **Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Agentic** layers.
* **16 Structured Training Templates:** Choose or customize programs across **Consistency Essentials**, **Heavy Duty Athletics**, **Conditioning & Recomp**, and **Delta Y Adaptive** series—or subscribe to community creator plans.
