# edgefit.app

**A social fitness application for tracking nutrition, training, and progress toward your goals.**

edgefit.app brings macro tracking, fitness logs, social motivation, and agentic AI together in one place. Its Delta Y analysis approach helps turn logged information into a clearer picture of progress, supporting more informed decisions as you work toward your goals.

## What edgefit.app brings together

- **Nutrition and macros:** Track macronutrients to understand how your nutrition aligns with your goals.
- **Fitness logging:** Record your training and review your fitness activity over time.
- **Delta Y analysis:** Use changes in your logged fitness data to help assess progress toward a goal and inform your next steps.
- **Agentic AI:** Bring nutrition and fitness information together to support goal-oriented analysis and guidance.
- **Social fitness:** Connect fitness tracking with a social experience that can help keep you motivated.

## How it fits together

Start with a goal, track your macros and fitness activity, and review your progress over time. Delta Y analysis helps make changes in your fitness logs meaningful in relation to that goal, while the AI-supported experience helps you consider your next steps. The social aspect adds a way to share the fitness journey with others.
